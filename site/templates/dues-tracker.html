Rhythm reminder (kept identical every cycle)

  1. Announce due date at meeting + report + usual club channel.
  2. Reminder on the due date — kind, no names.
  3. Two private nudges a week apart, if needed, then mark "o" and move on.
  4. Two+ cycles unpaid: kind private check-in ("everything OK?"), not collection.
  5. Persistent cases are a club policy question: facts to the officers, once.

Payment options the club offers (update to your reality)

  • Cash at the meeting — always counted by two people, and the payer gets a receipt.
  • Check to the club — receipt on request (or always; cheap).
  • [Your bank's electronic option] — record the confirmation alongside the payout.

Every payment is receipted to the payer, cash included. The tracker stays boring. Boring is winning.
